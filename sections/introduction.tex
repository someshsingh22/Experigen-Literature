\subsection{Persona Scaling — Open RQs, TODOs \& Ideas}

\paragraph{Research Questions}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{RQ1 (Persona Count):} How does the number of personas affect the diversity \emph{and} accuracy of generated hypotheses? What is the optimal number of personas?
    \item \textbf{RQ2 (Persona Detail):} How detailed should personas be for this task? Which attributes are most important? What is the best $(\text{size} \times \text{attributes})$ configuration?
    \emph{(RQ2 is harder and downstream of RQ1.)}
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Current Finding}
Personas improve hypothesis diversity; more target-population-informed personas yield better diversity. However, diversity $\neq$ accuracy — random personas would be diverse but inaccurate. Dataset knowledge is essential for accuracy.
